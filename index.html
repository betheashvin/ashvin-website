The short version

My first real taste of code was an online blockchain bootcamp where nothing felt familiar. By chapter nine I had to write everything myself — and I got stuck for days at a time. That's when it clicked: coding isn't understanding something once, it's getting stuck, debugging, and learning your way through. I've chased that feeling ever since.

Since then I've built AI agents, full-stack products, and a Web3 app on the same blockchain ideas that once left me lost. But what keeps me going isn't the stack — it's what the work is for. I teach the AI tools I use to students who don't have easy access to them, and I've grown one of Pakistan's largest student communities from 30,000 to over 100,000.

I'm a lifelong learner, still hunting for the next place to go deeper — happiest building things, with other people, that leave something better behind.
